## Shared hearing method

`summary_profiles.HEARING_READABILITY` supplies the full method to both accepted
profiles and unassigned compatibility work. The unassigned synthesis SKILL reinforces
it; the existing generic profile skill already delegates to the full runtime contract.
The generic skill, synthesis tool wrapper, base extraction extension and extraction
skills/contracts remain unchanged, avoiding irrelevant report/extraction invalidation.

The method now explicitly requires:

1. **Substantive opener.** Identify the purpose or consequential result and necessary
   qualifications before appearances, exhibit inventories or logistics. A proceeding
   without a decision should identify the pending issue, not invent an outcome.
   Preserve the difference between requests, tentatives and actual rulings; do not
   import a later hearing's result into an earlier event.
2. **Explain an argument once.** Use the overview to orient, not preview every position
   before repeating counsel-by-counsel accounts. Group shared positions but retain
   differences in grounds, requested relief, conditions and reservations. Keep short,
   focused paragraphs with as much total coverage as genuinely distinct issues need.
3. **Useful, grammatical quotations.** Prefer disputed premises, legal arguments,
   consequential conditions and reasons over stock appearances/submissions. Ordinary
   appearances and connective prose can remain paraphrase. Read expanded quoted words
   in their sentence; avoid doubled subjects/verbs, dangling fragments and duplicative
   paraphrase-plus-quote. Recast surrounding prose, choose another suitable anchor or
   paraphrase—never rewrite/shorten stored quotations or shift their speaker/actor/duty
   bearer. Source-exact words alone do not prove correct attribution.
4. **Consequential selection.** Preserve material legal findings, burdens, evidence
   limitations, notice disputes, conditions, review rights and operative dates. Give
   routine recitals/inventories less weight unless consequential. Do not convert
   conditional standard directions into unconditional findings/duties, assume their
   applicability, or omit their material practical effect.
5. **Conflicting accounts remain honest.** Reread relevant digest blocks/drafts before
   asserting a position or ruling. Explain only a supported change; otherwise attribute
   the specific unresolved discrepancy briefly. Counsel's description of a prior ruling
   is not an independently verified court finding. Do not guess at contradictory dates
   or invent missing names/pronouns. Synthesis still cannot consult source pages, so a
   digest-level discrepancy that it cannot resolve must not be silently corrected.

Fictional neutral examples teach orientation and grammatical integration; they are
not record facts, templates, length targets or extra evidence. All review occurs as
part of normal incremental synthesis. No word/paragraph quota, extra model pass,
new tool, blocking validator, repair loop or deterministic prose rewriting is added.
The preceding exact built-in remains recognized without attaching obsolete instructions
as custom guidance. Genuine custom prose stays byte-preserved and subordinate; accepted
profile topics/role data are unchanged.
