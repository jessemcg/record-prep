Classify one clerk's transcript (CT) page from an appellate record.
Compare all category descriptions and choose the single best-supported fit.

Allowed page_type values: ["CT_cover", "CT_index", "CT_minute_order", "CT_report", "CT_form", "CT_certificate", "CT_motion", "CT_notice", "CT_proof_of_service", "CT_other"].
Example response: {"page_type": "CT_cover"}

Category Descriptions

CT_cover
Cover page for the clerk's transcript or a section (case caption, title, etc.).

CT_index
Index/table of contents listing documents and page numbers.

CT_minute_order
Clerk-prepared summary of a hearing.
   Often labeled MINUTE ORDER.
   Look for: appearances list, orders issued, judge/bench officer, court reporter, department.
   No verbatim dialogue.
   Not prepared by an attorney.
   These DO show Page X of XX.

CT_report
Authored narrative or evaluative report prepared for the court, including continuation pages.
Look for a report heading, preparer or agency attribution, dated sections, findings, assessment or recommendations.
Footer report labels or codes such as CFS 6.7 and DCFS 4216 are supporting clues, not required names.
Distinguish reports from clerk-prepared minute orders, preprinted court forms, motions, notices and raw records or exhibits.
A report need not have an approved output name. Page X of XX numbering alone does not make it a form or exclude a report.

CT_form
Preprinted form with a form ID and Judicial Council approval in the footer.
   Form ID must be JV-XXX, FL-XXX, or ICWA-XXX.
   These forms DO show Page X of XX.

CT_certificate
Certificate executed by the clerk authenticating transcripts or documents.

CT_motion
A party's motion asking the court to act; may include points and authorities.

CT_notice
Formal notice of hearing date/time/nature.

CT_proof_of_service
Declaration showing a party was served (mail, personal, electronic).

CT_other
Anything else (medical records, drug tests, service logs, letters, exhibits, photos, text messages, emails, case plans, written court order, etc.).
