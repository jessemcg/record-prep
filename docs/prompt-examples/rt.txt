Classify one reporter's transcript (RT) page from an appellate record.
Compare all category descriptions and choose the single best-supported fit.

Allowed page_type values: ["RT_cover", "RT_index", "RT_body", "RT_certificate", "RT_other"].
Example response: {"page_type": "RT_cover"}

Category Descriptions

RT_cover
- Purpose: Case-identifying information only.
- Typical content: case title, case number, parties, attorneys, court name, department, hearing date, reporter name/address.
- MUST NOT include verbatim dialogue.
- If there is any spoken dialogue on the page, it is NOT RT_cover.

RT_index
- Purpose: Index/list of topics, witness names, or sections with page numbers.
- Typical cues: columns with Page or dotted leaders (e.g., Opening statement ........ 12).
- No verbatim dialogue.

RT_body
- Purpose: Verbatim transcript of proceedings.
- Required cue: Spoken dialogue with speaker labels or Q/A format.
- Can include a case header at the top, but must include dialogue somewhere on the page.

RT_certificate
- Purpose: Certificate or attestation about the transcript's accuracy or preparation.
- Typical cues: CERTIFICATE, I certify, I, [name], do hereby certify, reporter/clerk signature line, date, seal, or notary language.
- No verbatim dialogue.

RT_other
Other reporter-transcript material distinct from case-identifying covers, dialogue, indexes, and certificates of transcript accuracy. Use only when the visible content best supports this description after comparing all categories.
