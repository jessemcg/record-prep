## Canonical skill and editorial method

`.pi/skills/recordprep-synthesize-reports/SKILL.md` now explicitly:

- Respects reader time: lead with the main **attributed** conclusion, recommendation,
  significant development or unresolved issue, with necessary qualification, then
  supporting evidence/reasons. Filing/caption metadata is not the default opener.
- Keeps paragraphs focused; topics organize extraction, not mandatory narrative blocks.
  Each fact gets its fullest treatment once, not repeated as evidence, barrier,
  progress and recent development. The opener orients rather than previewing an inventory.
- Emphasizes meaningful changes across reports; repeats earlier history/recommendations
  only where needed to explain the current assessment. Omit routine recitals and
  negative checklists unless consequential, without suppressing a material issue.
- Preserves favorable observations, counterevidence, conditions, uncertainty and source
  disagreements; reread relevant digests/drafts and never invent a reconciliation.
  Recommendations, allegations and historical recitals do not become current findings.
- Uses meaningful source-exact quotation anchors rather than stock formulas. Read
  expanded quotations as complete sentences; avoid doubled subjects/verbs, names,
  modifiers, dangling fragments and paraphrase/quote duplication. Preserve speaker,
  actor and duty bearer. Choose another anchor or paraphrase; never repair stored quotes.
- Includes short fictional orientation/quotation examples, not user case facts.
  No fixed length/outcome cap, bullets, extra pass, repair/tuning loop or blocking
  writing-quality validator. Keep the existing dataset tools, protocol feedback,
  source discipline, scratchpad and incremental submission method.
- Adds no capitalization/editorial announcement and does not attempt to repair worklist
  selection by inventing titles, merging fragments or skipping non-null documents.
