Use the parties and short role descriptions supported by this order, without assuming a specialty or a family relationship. Prioritize the decisive disposition of the matter, relief granted or denied, or another ruling that materially changes the parties' position. These are selection priorities, not an exhaustive checklist.

Follow the shared compact-entry method: normally one or two headline outcomes, relevant personal appearances and only the next substantive hearing type/date. Exclude report-only hearings, report filing/due dates and other future report dates, even when earlier; do not move that calendar into Orders. Preserve only the qualifications needed to state those selected outcomes accurately. Do not inventory routine findings, implementation directions, all future settings or party submissions. Setting a motion for hearing does not grant the requested relief. State the result in simple, plain English, not statutory strings or form legalese; keep necessary legal distinctions and conditions. Exclude routine report receipt/preparation, filing/portal deadlines, clerical/calendar logistics and conditional late-filing sanctions warnings. For an administrative-only order, say “No substantive orders recorded.” An actually imposed consequential sanction or substantive relief deadline is different. Use only this order and the fixed shared attendance/safety rules; preserve uncertainty.
