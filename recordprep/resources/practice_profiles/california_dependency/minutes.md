For dependency minute orders, prioritize the hearing's decisive change in custody/placement, reunification services, parental rights or permanent plan. A consequential visitation or ICWA/heritage ruling may instead be the headline. These are alternatives to guide selection, not a checklist to cover at every hearing.

Normally select one or two headline outcomes under the shared compact-entry method. Preserve a condition essential to the selected result, such as release conditioned on remaining in treatment. Do not reproduce all service referrals, routine dependency findings, implementation directions or unchanged orders. State those outcomes in simple, plain English: for example, whether the child returns home, services continue/end, or parental rights end. Preserve legally important distinctions, who is affected and an essential condition; do not reproduce statutory/form language. A hearing granted on a section 388 petition is not a grant of the requested modification. Exclude routine report receipt/preparation, filing/portal deadlines, clerical/calendar logistics and conditional late-filing sanctions warnings. For an administrative-only order, say “No substantive orders recorded.” Do not omit an actually imposed consequential sanction or a deadline essential to substantive relief.

Appearances focus on the individually identified parents; include a child, caregiver or other participant only when relevant to this order's purpose. Do not expand counsel listings into personal attendance or add a roster of court staff. Keep uncertainty explicit. Next identifies only the next substantive hearing and its date. Exclude report-receipt hearings, report filing/due dates and other future report dates, even when earlier; do not move that calendar into Orders.

Fictional examples (not case facts):

March 6, 2030 — Review
Appearances: Mother present; father's appearance unclear.
Orders: Mother will continue receiving services to help reunify with the child.
Next: Review September 5.

September 5, 2030 — Disposition
Appearances: Mother present; father absent.
Orders: Child placed outside the parents' custody. Mother will receive reunification services if she stays in residential treatment; father will not receive those services.
Next: Review March 6, 2031.

Use the order alone and the fixed shared attendance/safety rules. No separate party-position narrative or reporting-status sentence. Do not add facts to imitate these examples.
