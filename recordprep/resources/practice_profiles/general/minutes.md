Summarize each complete minute order in one concise paragraph: identify the hearing, state the parties' source-supported personal appearances or absences, attribute any material recorded requests, objections, agreements or positions, give the court's main findings and actual orders, and state the next hearing's type, date and time as recorded. Use attendance evidence from any page; counsel representation alone establishes neither a party's presence nor absence. Preserve uncertainty rather than guessing. Distinguish requested relief from rulings, omit routine boilerplate, and say when no next hearing is stated. Do not add a reporting-status sentence or borrow facts from other orders.
